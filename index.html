Focus Areas

Research interests

🧬

RNA Design and Structure Prediction

Deep learning approaches for modeling and designing RNA 3D structures within the Zhang Lab ecosystem at NUS.

🔤

Transformer Models in Genomics

Treating genomes as language - attention-based architectures for miRNA, TF binding and methylation discovery in plants.

🌿

Plant RNA & Epigenomics

Generative AI for condition-specific DNA methylation; miRNA biogenesis; lncRNA discovery across plant genomes.

⚡

Generative AI for Biology

Encoder-decoder generative frameworks (DMRU, PTF-Vāc, GANGA) for sequence generation and annotation tasks.

🏗️

TF Binding & Regulation

Universal cross-species TF binding region and site discovery using co-learning on TF 3D structure and ChIP/DAP-seq data.

🖥️

HPC & GPU Computing

Large-scale genomics on HPC clusters; system maintenance (IPMI & iDRAC); PBS and Slurm job management; NGS data analysis.
